# **Safety & Scam Awareness Guide**

## **1\. General Safety Tips**

* Always verify the identity of anyone you plan to room with or share transportation with.  
* Use video calls (Zoom, Teams, FaceTime) before committing to sharing accommodations.  
* Ask for institutional affiliation (e.g., university, research group) and verify it independently.  
* Meet in a public place (such as the conference venue) before traveling together or exchanging keys.  
* Trust your instincts — if a conversation feels off, disengage.

## **2\. Protecting Your Personal Information**

* Do not share your home address, passport details, or financial information with other users.  
* Use your professional or institutional email rather than a personal one if possible.  
* Avoid sending photos of IDs or travel documents.  
* Do not make payments or reimbursements until you have confirmed details with the other person.

## **3\. Recognizing Scam Behavior**

Be cautious if someone:

* Avoids video calls or claims their camera is “broken.”  
* Pressures you to commit quickly or demands payment up front.  
* Sends unusually low-cost offers that seem too good to be true.  
* Asks for gift cards, cryptocurrency, wire transfers, or unusual payment methods.  
* Claims an “emergency” that requires immediate money.

If any of these occur, do not proceed.

## **4\. Money & Payment Safety**

* Whenever possible, make hotel or car reservations through official websites where costs can be split at check-in.  
* If sharing costs (such as an Airbnb), make sure all names are on the reservation when possible.  
* Use secure and traceable payment methods (Venmo, PayPal, or bank transfer), not cash or gift cards.  
* Keep screenshots or receipts of all agreements.

## **5\. Meeting & Rooming Safety**

* Choose hotels that have 24/7 staff and security.  
* Share your travel plans with a colleague or friend.  
* When you first meet your roommate, do so in a public, well-lit area.  
* Lock valuables in your luggage or use hotel safes.  
* Set clear expectations ahead of time (e.g., sleeping schedule, visitors, noise, shared rides).
